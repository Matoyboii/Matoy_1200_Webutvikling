About the Park

The park was founded by Dio in 1994. Dio wanted to make a place where everyone can coexist and forget about the plights of the world, and through the advent of Mako Energy, his dreams could be fulfilled.

Attractions!

The park is split into 7 squares with tons of different attraction!

Battle Square

Join the Musclehead Colosseum in the Battle Square to fight to your heart's content.
Fight monsters in unique challenges to name yourself champion. Or stay a spectator if you're a coward.


Chocobo Square

Become a chocobo jockey and race in fun chocobo races. Or bet on your favorite jockey to wins tons of gil!


Event Square

Take a break from the action at the Event Square, where you can watch emotional plays like Loveless.
Or join our special plays that take audience participation to the next level through modern VR technology.


Ghost Square

Stay the night at the Haunted Hotel for an unforgettable night. If the immaculate ambience doesn't give you a restless night, then we don't know what will!

Just stay alert around robot humidifiers:)

Book rooms on the menu page.

Skywheel Square

Get intimate at the Skywheel. As you ride, experience the views and wonders of the Gold Saucer. You might glimpse familiar faces flying around you with the help of hologram technology.
We often host couple's nights, so bring a partner for a truly intimate and romantic journey.

Speed Square

Join the forces of Gilgamesh to defeat his enemies in this epic ride through space.

Wonderment Square

Down for some arcade fun?
At the wonderment square there is something for everybody!

Go on the G-bike for some hack n' slash fun.


Or win over your friends in 3D-Brawler!
And much more! There's also Snowboarding and Queen's Blood events!
